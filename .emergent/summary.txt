<original_problem_statement>
The user wants to build a comprehensive, three-sided mobile application for their talent agency, A Few Good Men Casting.

**PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS:**

1.  **Talent Portal:**
    *   Talent can log in to a personal profile.
    *   Manage personal details, physical stats (height, weight, shoe size), and photos.
    *   An availability calendar to mark days they are unavailable.
    *   View current availability checks and respond (Yes/No/Maybe).
    *   View current and past job bookings.
    *   View payment history with detailed breakdowns (chits).
    *   Query pay and have it amended by admins.

2.  **Production Portal:**
    *   Productions can log in to browse the talent database.
    *   Filter talent based on various criteria (height, sex, etc.).
    *   Select talent using a Tinder-style swipe interface (thumbs up to select).
    *   Create production projects, add dates, and specify job details.

3.  **Admin/Back Office Portal:**
    *   View incoming jobs and selections from productions.
    *   Manage and assist with payment chits and talent financing.
    *   Liaise with productions to select and send availability checks to talent.
    *   A built-in messaging system for communication with talent and productions.
    *   Ability to send broadcast messages to all talents with a specific skill.
    *   Manage and update predetermined pay rates (e.g., FAA, PACT).
    *   Ability to edit talent's star ratings as well as any profile. Also ability to delete and approve photos. 

4.  **Referral & Quality Control System:**
    *   Talent sign-up is by referral only. The main sign-up page should be locked.
    *   New talents must enter two 4-digit referral codes from two different existing talents to unlock the sign-up page.
    *   Existing talents can generate and share these 4-digit codes from their dashboard.
    *   The system must track who referred whom.
    *   A 5-star rating system for each talent, visible to admins and productions.
    *   New talents start with 3 stars.
    *   Admins can adjust the star ratings.

5.  **Branding & UI:**
    *   The company logo should be on the splash screen and in the header of all pages.
    *   The initial splash screen should have three main buttons: Admin, Talent, and Production.

</original_problem_statement>
